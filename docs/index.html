How a pick is made

  1. Screen the market

    Start from every ASX listing. Keep stocks with 3+ years of history, a price of at least 25c and $500k+ average daily turnover.

  2. Forecast with TimesFM

    Feed each stock's last two years of daily closes to TimesFM 2.5, a pretrained time-series model. It returns a median path plus a 10th–90th percentile range.

  3. Rank by edge per unit of risk

    Score = forecast return ÷ width of the forecast range. Only forecasts that beat round-trip slippage qualify.

  4. Trade and repeat

    Buy the top picks at the next open in equal dollar amounts, sell at the close after the holding period, then rebalance.

Read before trading

  • Not financial advice. This is a personal research project. Picks are model output, not recommendations to buy.
  • Past results do not predict future returns. Six months is a short window; a strategy can beat the index by luck.
  • Survivorship bias. The backtest only includes companies listed today, so stocks that collapsed and delisted are missing. Real results would likely be worse.
  • Costs scale with account size. Brokerage is a fixed fee per order, so small accounts lose a far larger share to fees than the capital shown here.
  • Prices are end-of-day from Yahoo Finance and can be late or wrong. Check the price before placing an order.
