How it works

    Plain-language terms

    Read before using

    • Not financial advice. A personal research project; results are simulations.
    • This program places no trades. "LIVE" is a research tier, not a broker connection. Share quantities use adjusted research prices and are not executable.
    • Past results do not predict future returns. July 2024 onwards has already been examined, so it is historical evidence, not an untouched test.
    • Survivorship bias. Only companies listed today are included, so real results would likely be worse.
    • Estimated tax. The tax overlay is a rough capital-gains estimate; dividend tax and franking are ignored.
    • Prices are end-of-day from Yahoo Finance and can be late or wrong.
