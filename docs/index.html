    How the recommendation is made

    1. Screen the market

      Each month-end, keep the 250 most-traded ASX stocks priced at $1+ with $2m+ median daily turnover and clean price history. ETFs are excluded.

    2. Score 22 strategy variants

      Risk-adjusted 12-month momentum, low volatility, a blend of both, and two TimesFM signals. Each uses 5 or 8 positions, with or without an All Ords 200-day trend filter.

    3. Trade slowly to cut costs

      Rebalance monthly. Buy only top-N names, keep them until they fall below rank 3N, never top up, and skip orders under $1,000.

    4. Prove it out of sample

      Pick the variant on 2016–2024 data, test it on the untouched 2024–2026 hold-out, and apply deflated Sharpe and overfitting checks. If anything fails, recommend the index ETF.

    Read before trading

    • Not financial advice. This is a personal research project. Picks are model output, not recommendations to buy.
    • Past results do not predict future returns. Six months is a short window; a strategy can beat the index by luck.
    • Survivorship bias. The backtest only includes companies listed today, so stocks that collapsed and delisted are missing. Real results would likely be worse.
    • Costs scale with account size. Brokerage is a fixed fee per order, so small accounts lose a far larger share to fees than the capital shown here.
    • Prices are end-of-day from Yahoo Finance and can be late or wrong. Check the price before placing an order.
